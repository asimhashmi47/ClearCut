# Product

## Register

brand (landing page), product (studio view)

## Users

People who need a subject isolated from an image: online sellers, designers, developers, and anyone preparing a profile photo. They want a result in seconds, and many are put off by sign-ups, credits, watermarks, or uploading personal photos to someone else's server.

## Product Purpose

Clearcut is a free, open source background remover. The model runs in the visitor's browser, so images are never uploaded. It ships as a static site that anyone can host or fork.

## Brand Personality

Loud, practical, and honest. The identity comes from the film set: gaffer-tape labels, a scan line, and the transparency checkerboard showing wherever a background has been removed. Colors come from two Color Hunt palettes: `F4F0E2 F4BAA3 A6C8E2 619DC6` for light (with a deep navy derived from `619DC6` for text) and `091413 285A48 408A71 B0E4CC` for dark.

## Anti-references

Do not use another product's name, branding, or copy. Avoid the category's blue-and-white SaaS look, purple gradients, and dark terminal aesthetics. Never claim a result the model did not produce: showcase images must be real, unedited output (see `scripts/generate-samples.mjs`).

## Design Principles

- Choosing an image is the first and most obvious action.
- Let real cutouts prove the quality. Visitors can drag the divider and inspect the edges.
- State every privacy claim in a way the visitor can check for themselves.
- Keep copy direct and specific about what the tool does.
- Make states understandable without relying on color or motion alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation and visible focus, maintain accessible text contrast in light and dark themes, provide meaningful image descriptions, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
